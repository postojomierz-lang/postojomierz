PLASTIC FRONT

Tip out the toy box. Spend your supply on soldiers, vehicles, aircraft and fortifications, then watch the plastic armies fight it out. Each round brings reinforcements. The last headquarters standing wins.

Your description or photo is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to lay out the map. The key is saved only in this browser; use a key with a spending limit. If anything fails you get a random battlefield instead.

Online battles against other players come in a later stage. Right now the other armies are commanded by the computer.

Controls

CameraLeft-drag or WASD to pan · right-drag or Q/E to rotate · wheel or pinch to zoom BuildPick a piece at the bottom, click or drag inside your zone · R rotates walls · right-click removes a piece placed this round · Ctrl+Z undo BattleSpace starts the battle / pauses · 1, 2, 4 change speed RoundsEach battle lasts 40 seconds. Survivors stay where they are; you then get more supply to reinforce. Up to 3 vehicles and 2 aircraft per round. AircraftFighters hunt aircraft, attack planes hit tanks and guns, bombers make one bombing run near the nearest enemy HQ, transports drop 6 paratroopers. Counter them with anti-air guns, AA missile soldiers and your own fighters. WoundedSoldiers often go down wounded instead of dying (red cross). A nearby comrade drags them behind cover or home, and field ambulances carry up to 4 back to base. Anyone saved fights again next round for free. Wounded left out in the open bleed out. Friendly fireExplosions hurt everyone nearby, including your own soldiers. Troops avoid lobbing grenades or shells at enemies standing next to friends, but misses happen. MinimapClick the minimap (bottom right) to jump the camera there. WinningDestroy the enemy headquarters. After 10 rounds the strongest army wins.
