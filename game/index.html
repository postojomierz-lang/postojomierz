PLASTIC FRONT

Tip out the toy box. Spend your supply on soldiers, vehicles and fortifications, then watch the plastic armies fight it out. Each round brings reinforcements. The last headquarters standing wins.

Online battles against other players come in a later stage. Right now the other armies are commanded by the computer.
