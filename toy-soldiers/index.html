How to play

You command the green army. Each round, every soldier may move, then fire (firing ends its turn). Then the tan army moves.

RiflemanReliable, two shots. Machine gunnerFour shots, only two if it moved first. BazookaBig single hit; wrecks tanks. SniperVery long range, ignores half of cover. Fragile. GrenadierLobs over walls, ignores cover, splashes neighbours (friends too!). OfficerAllies within 2 tiles aim +10%. TankTough and armoured: small arms do 1 less damage.
WallsBooks, bricks, boxes: block movement and line of sight. Low coverCrayons, slippers, sandbags: −30% to hit, slow to cross. Standing next to cover also helps. Pillows & moundsHigh ground: +10% aim, +1 range. SpillsImpassable, but you can shoot across. ClutterLEGO pieces, crumbs: slow to cross.

Keys: E end turn · W hold · Esc deselect. When the round limit hits, the kids get called for dinner and the battle is over.
