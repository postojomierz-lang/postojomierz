PLASTIC FRONT

Tip out the toy box. Spend your supply on soldiers, vehicles, aircraft and fortifications, then watch the plastic armies fight it out. Each round brings reinforcements. The last headquarters standing wins.

Draw on white paper with markers: black = books, boxes and walls · red = LEGO bricks · blue = spills · green lines = low cover (pencils). Keep the army zones empty, then photograph just the sheet from above. Everything is read on your device; nothing is uploaded.

Advanced: let Claude design the battlefield (needs your own Anthropic API key)

Your description or photo is sent to Anthropic's Claude API and billed to your key. The key is saved only in this browser; use one with a spending limit. If anything fails you get a random battlefield instead.

Online battles against other players come in a later stage. Right now the other armies are commanded by the computer.

Controls

CameraLeft-drag or WASD to pan · right-drag or Q/E to rotate · wheel or pinch to zoom BuildPick a piece at the bottom, click or drag inside your zone · R rotates walls · right-click removes a piece placed this round · Ctrl+Z undo BattleSpace starts the battle / pauses · 1, 2, 4 change speed RoundsEach battle lasts 40 seconds. Survivors stay where they are; you then get more supply to reinforce. Up to 3 vehicles and 2 aircraft per round. AircraftFighters hunt aircraft, attack planes hit tanks and guns, bombers make one bombing run near the nearest enemy HQ, transports drop 6 paratroopers. Counter them with anti-air guns, AA missile soldiers and your own fighters. OrdersEach round pick a main target (the drop-down, or click an army in the list on the right): your vehicles, bombers and most of your infantry march on that army (every fourth soldier stays home to guard the HQ), but still shoot anyone who comes in range, and turn back if your base is invaded. Defend keeps everyone inside your zone to guard the HQ. The list also shows who every army is going for. WoundedSoldiers often go down wounded instead of dying (red cross). A nearby comrade drags them behind the closest cover (a book, a mug, sandbags), medics patch them up on the spot so they fight on, and field ambulances pick up to 4 from safe spots and drive them back to base — they steer clear of enemy fire. Anyone saved fights again next round for free. Wounded left out in the open bleed out. Friendly fireExplosions hurt everyone nearby, including your own soldiers. Troops avoid lobbing grenades or shells at enemies standing next to friends, but misses happen. MapsPlay a random battlefield, pick one from the map library, load a map file a friend sent you, or draw one on paper and photograph it. "Save map" (top bar) saves the current battlefield; the Map editor lets you build your own. MinimapClick the minimap (bottom right) to jump the camera there. WinningDestroy the enemy headquarters. After 10 rounds the strongest army wins.
